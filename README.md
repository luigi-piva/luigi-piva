## Luigi Piva, CQF

Systematic portfolio manager and quantitative researcher — mid-frequency futures and FX.
Founder of **AlgosWorks AI**; applied research published through **Quanthedge AI**.

My work centres on one question: how to build portfolios of strategies that
survive out of sample. Signal research, validation discipline (hold-out,
deflated Sharpe, nested cross-validation), regime and market-stress measurement,
and multi-strategy allocation — through to live execution.

**Research areas**
- Mid-frequency systematic strategies on futures and FX
- Strategy validation: backtest overfitting, deflated Sharpe, purged cross-validation
- Regime and market-stress measurement (turbulence, absorption, exposure scaling)
- Multi-strategy allocation and portfolio construction
- Derivatives pricing and numerical methods
